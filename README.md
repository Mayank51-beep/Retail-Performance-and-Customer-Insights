# 🛍️ Retail Performance & Customer Insights Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

An end-to-end **Retail Performance & Customer Insights** analysis built using **Microsoft Excel** to evaluate sales performance, customer behaviour, product and country performance, returns, and profitability scenarios.

The project combines **data cleaning, Power Query, PivotTables, RFM customer segmentation, interactive dashboards, and What-If analysis** to convert raw retail transaction data into actionable business insights.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyse overall **retail sales and revenue performance**
* Identify **top-performing products and countries**
* Analyse **monthly sales trends**
* Measure **return/cancellation rates**
* Segment customers using **RFM analysis**
* Identify valuable, loyal, and at-risk customers
* Evaluate the impact of different **discount factors on profit**
* Build an interactive dashboard for business decision-making

---

## 🗂️ Project Workflow

**Raw Retail Data → Data Cleaning → Data Transformation → Sales Analysis → RFM Segmentation → What-If Analysis → Interactive Dashboard → Business Insights**

## 🧹 1. Data Preparation & ETL

The retail transaction dataset was prepared using **Excel Power Query**.

### Key tasks performed:

* Removed/handled unnecessary and invalid records
* Managed missing Customer IDs
* Identified cancelled transactions using invoice numbers beginning with `C`
* Created/standardized date-related fields
* Created **Year, Month and Year-Month** dimensions
* Calculated revenue-related metrics
* Prepared separate data logic for sales analysis and return-rate analysis

The cleaned dataset was structured into an Excel Table (`tblSales`) for analysis and PivotTables.

## 👥 3. RFM Customer Segmentation

RFM analysis was performed at the **customer level** using:

### Recency

Number of days since the customer's last purchase.

### Frequency

Number of distinct invoices/purchases made by the customer.

### Monetary

Total revenue generated by the customer.

Each RFM metric was divided into **quintile-based scores from 1–5**.

* **R Score:** Lower recency days = higher score
* **F Score:** Higher purchase frequency = higher score
* **M Score:** Higher monetary value = higher score

The three scores were combined into an **RFM Score**, such as `5-4-5`.

Customers were then grouped into business-oriented segments such as:

* 👑 Champions
* 🤝 Loyal Customers
* 🌱 Potential Loyalists
* ⚠️ At Risk
* 💤 Inactive

The RFM Group was also connected back to transaction-level sales data to enable interactive dashboard filtering.

## 💡 5. What-If Profit Scenario

A separate **What-If** sheet was created to understand how changes in the revenue factor affect profitability.

### Scenario Model

**Adjusted Revenue = Revenue × Discount Factor**

Gross Profit was calculated using an assumed **25% profit margin**.

Discount/revenue factors from **0.90 to 1.10** were tested using Excel's:

**Data → What-If Analysis → Data Table**

A line chart, **“Discount Factor vs. Profit”**, visualizes the relationship between the factor and Gross Profit.

### Business Recommendation

The analysis demonstrates that changes in the factor directly affect adjusted revenue and gross profit. Therefore, discounts should be applied selectively rather than broadly. Targeted discounts can be used for specific customers, products or promotional periods while maintaining sufficient margins to protect profitability.
